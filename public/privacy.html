Privacy & analytics

Updated October 26, 2026

Your conversion inputs

Calculations run in your browser. We do not send conversion inputs, amounts, product specifications or copied results to Google Analytics. Preferences such as language and tool order are saved in your browser's local storage. Currency rates are requested from Frankfurter using currency pairs; amounts stay in your browser.

Website analytics

UniPort uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, traffic sources and interactions such as page views, scrolling and outbound link clicks. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes information such as page URLs, browser and device details, approximate location and usage events. Google Signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled in our website configuration. Local development and preview domains do not load our analytics tag.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, read Google's privacy policy, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can manage or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

隐私与访问统计

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

换算在浏览器内完成。换算输入、金额、商品规格与复制结果不会发送至 Google Analytics。语言、工具排序等偏好保存在浏览器本地存储中。汇率工具仅向 Frankfurter 请求货币对，金额留在本地。

UniPort 使用 Google Analytics 4 了解访问量、流量来源、页面浏览、滚动和出站链接点击等互动。Google Analytics 使用 Cookie，并处理页面网址、浏览器与设备信息、大致位置及使用事件。网站配置已关闭 Google Signals 和广告个性化信号。本地开发和预览域名不会加载本站统计代码。

你可以查看 Google 如何使用合作网站的信息和 Google 隐私政策，使用 Google Analytics 停用插件，或通过浏览器设置管理、删除 Cookie 和本地存储。
